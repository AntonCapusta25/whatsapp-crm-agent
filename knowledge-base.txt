Homemade — Full System Overview (Chatbot Knowledge Base)
This document is a complete reference of the Homemade platform: business model, plans, commissions, onboarding flow, verification, compliance, fees, and operational rules. It is written so it can be fed directly to a chatbot as its knowledge source.

1. What is Homemade?
Homemade is a platform that connects hobby chefs with customers in their local community. Chefs cook from their home kitchens and sell via delivery and/or pickup through the Homemade storefront (powered internally by Hyperzod).

Company: Homemade B.V.
Address: Klokkenplas 40-1, 7511 NN Enschede, Netherlands
Active cities: Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Enschede, Haarlem, Den Haag
Booking / launch assistance: https://calendly.com/homemademeals-info/launch-assistance (with Tia, onboarding specialist)
Relationship: Chefs are independent contractors on a hobby basis — not employees of Homemade.

2. Service Plans & Commissions
Three tiers, two billing options. Yearly is the default (commission-only, lower long-term cost). Monthly adds a base fee with a lower commission.

Yearly plans (commission only)
- Basic Chef: 10% commission, 2 h consulting/month, up to 8 menu items, 1 promo video. Standard branding, KVK/NVWA & menu/pricing support, dashboard, invoicing.
- Pro Chef: 12% commission, 4 h consulting/month, unlimited menu items, 2 promo videos. Enhanced branding, 1 social feature/month, training sessions, priority support, performance coaching.
- Pro Chef Plus (Advanced): 14% commission, 6+ h consulting/month, unlimited menu items, 3+ promo videos. Premium branding, 3 social features/month, all trainings, B2B catering access, priority positioning, advanced analytics, dedicated account manager, homepage features & premium badges.

Monthly plans (base fee + commission)
- Basic Chef (Monthly): €25 / month + 5% commission.
- Pro Chef (Monthly): €35 / month + 6% commission.
- Pro Chef Plus (Monthly): €45 / month + 7% commission.

Commission base:
Commission is taken from the gross order value, which includes delivery fees and VAT.

Term & renewal:
- Yearly plans: 12-month term from signing date, auto-renewing.
- Monthly plans: 1-month term, auto-renewing, 30-day written cancellation notice.
- Upgrade/Downgrade: Chefs can upgrade at any time. Pro and Pro Plus can downgrade at any time.

Internal commission note (Hyperzod):
For the storefront backend, all merchants are created with a nested commission structure of 15% across delivery, pickup, and custom_1 order types (this is the platform-side default validation — the chef-facing commission is the plan rate above).

3. Fees, Cancellations & Penalties
Approachable tone ("Platform Fees & Charges"):
- Order cancellation policy:
  * Within the first 10 minutes: No fee. Chef must personally contact the customer to explain.
  * After 10 minutes: €15 handling fee applies. Chef is expected to make it right (e.g. a complimentary meal on a future order).
  * 3+ cancellations within 3 months: Homemade schedules a support call and may temporarily pause new orders.
- Other:
  * No platform subscription fees on yearly plans (commission only).
  * Monthly plans charge the base fee at the start of each billing period.

4. Legal & Compliance Requirements
All chefs must comply with Dutch hobby-chef regulations:
- HACCP: Food safety compliance is mandatory.
- NVWA: Registration with the Dutch Food Safety Authority is mandatory.
- KVK: Chamber of Commerce registration (8-digit KVK number) required for operating. Status options during onboarding: KVK + NVWA both, KVK only, none yet, "try first".
- Liability insurance: Strongly recommended, minimum €1,000,000 per occurrence (general + professional liability).

5. Onboarding Flow (Chef-facing, Typeform-style wizard)
Steps in order:
1. Welcome
2. City
3. Cuisine(s)
4. Contact (email + phone — Dutch mobiles normalized to +316XXXXXXXX)
5. Address
6. Business name (AI-generated or manual)
7. Logo (AI / upload / placeholder)
8. Service type (delivery / pickup / both / unsure)
9. Availability buckets (weekday eves, weekend eves, lunch, flexible)
10. Dish types
11. Food safety status (has certificate / needs training)
12. KVK / NVWA status
13. Plan selection
14. Summary / account creation (silent magic-link auth)
State is persisted in localStorage; returning users with known emails are resumed via magic link (token_hash flow, rate-limited).

6. Verification Flow (after onboarding)
Mandatory sequence — do not reorder:
1. Menu Review: AI-generated menu (max 5 mains + culturally appropriate extras: bread/wraps, starches, non-alcoholic drinks, desserts). Chef approves / edits. Smart Merge preserves manually uploaded images on later edits.
2. Logo Review
3. Kitchen Check: Chef uploads 2 kitchen photos + 1 fridge photo. Gemini AI analyzes via structured tool call.
   * Scoring bands: 95–100 Commercial/Pristine · 70–84 Good Home (Pass) · 55–69 Needs Improvement (Conditional) · <55 Fail
   * Categories scored independently: Hygiene, Fridge Safety, Storage.
   * Monthly re-checks managed at /kitchen-check route via magic-link.
4. Food Safety Quiz: 5 mandatory training videos + Typeform quiz. Pass = ≥80%. Video progress saved in localStorage per chef. Passing scores shown so chefs don't retake.
5. Upload ID (and KVK / HACCP / NVWA documents)
6. Contract signing: typed signature + Terms + Privacy checkboxes
7. Congrats screen
Skipping food safety triggers an urgent admin alert.

7. Admin Onboarding Pipeline (Internal, 7 steps)
Auto-generated when a chef enters "Onboarding In Progress". Tasks cascade off verification.created_at.
Steps:
1. Verification (2 days)
2. Brand Review (1 day)
3. KVK Name Verification (admin only) (1 day)
4. Menu (5 days)
5. Merchant Profile (1 day)
6. Launch Prep (5 days)
7. Launch & Social Assets (7 days)
Only the current active step appears in global task lists. is_task_paused hides a chef's tasks globally.

8. "Online" vs "Offline" Storefront Status
A chef/merchant shows as Online (green) only when both:
- is_accepting_orders = true
- Within current opening hours (is_open = true)
Otherwise: Offline (gray). Terminology is "Online/Offline" everywhere, not "Open/Closed".

9. Merchant Defaults (Storefront / Hyperzod)
- Created inactive / offline (status = 0) by default.
- Delivery radius: 2.5 km (2,500 m) default. Hyperzod API often ignores this and falls back to 20,000 km — must be adjusted manually in the Hyperzod Merchant Panel.
- Tenant: X-TENANT 3331.
- Merchant data is fetched fresh from the database at the moment of Hyperzod creation.

17. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q: What does it cost to be on Homemade?
A: You can choose a yearly commission-only plan (10%, 12%, or 14%) or a monthly plan that combines a small base fee (€25/€35/€45) with a lower commission (5%/6%/7%). Yearly is the default.

Q: What's the commission based on?
A: Gross order value, including delivery and VAT.

Q: What happens if I cancel an order?
A: Within the first 10 minutes — no fee, but contact the customer. After 10 minutes — a €15 handling fee, and we ask you to make it right (e.g. a free meal next order). 3+ cancellations in 3 months triggers a support call and may pause orders.

Q: Do I need a KVK / NVWA / HACCP?
A: Yes — KVK + NVWA registration and HACCP compliance are mandatory. We also recommend at least €1,000,000 liability insurance.

Q: How long is the contract?
A: Yearly plans run for 12 months from signing. Monthly plans renew each month with 30 days' notice.

Q: Can I switch plans?
A: Yes — you can upgrade any time. Pro and Pro Plus can also downgrade.

Q: Can I get help during onboarding?
A: Yes — book a call with Tia, our onboarding specialist: https://calendly.com/homemademeals-info/launch-assistance

Q: How does the kitchen check work?
A: Upload 2 kitchen photos + 1 fridge photo. Our AI scores Hygiene, Fridge Safety, and Storage. 70+ passes; 55–69 conditional; <55 fail. Re-check monthly.

Q: What food-safety quiz score do I need?
A: 80% or higher. You'll watch 5 short training videos first.

Q: What does "Online" mean on the storefront?
A: You're Online (green) only when you're accepting orders AND within your opening hours. Otherwise you're Offline (gray).
